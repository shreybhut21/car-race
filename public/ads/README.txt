Put your custom sponsor and advertisement images in this folder!

Example:
1. Place an image here named "sponsor_banner.png" (e.g., 1920x1080 or 1024x512 PNG/JPG).
2. In src/track/TrackLandmarks.js, set:
   imageSrc: '/ads/sponsor_banner.png'
3. The game will automatically display your image directly on the 3D roadside billboards!
